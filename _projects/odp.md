## Project Overview

<p style="max-width: 1000px; line-height: 1.6; margin: 0 auto 1.5rem auto;">
  Save the Grapes is a vineyard-protection concept designed to divert spotted
  lanternflies away from real grape vines by presenting an artificial alternative
  target. Across these milestone pages, the project develops from initial pitch to
  working prototype to final exhibit and client-facing report.
</p>
